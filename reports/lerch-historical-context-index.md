## Working Chronology

- **Nerib-Hyêni:** If Temir Beg is the documented Timur/Temir Bey of Hani, the cleanest working range is c. 1819-1835: active as Hani notable by 1819 and defeated/exiled in 1835. This is a working historical identification, not a final date.
- **Sivan-Nyêrib:** The story appears to allude to the earlier Nerib-Hyêni conflict, so it likely follows that feud. The broad working range remains roughly 1820-1853, with 1853/Crimean War collection context as the upper horizon.
- **Ali Ağa:** No firm external date has been identified yet. Treat as a local feud narrative from the same remembered historical world, but do not attach it to the Temir Beg date range without evidence.
- **Hassan interview:** Not a feud event narrative; it is direct source/context evidence for Hassan, Sivan villages, and the feuds Hassan says he witnessed.
