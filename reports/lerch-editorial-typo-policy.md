# Lerch Editorial Typo Policy

Working policy for suspected errors in Lerch's printed Zazaki transcription:

1. Preserve Lerch's printed form in the interlinear `LERCH` row.
2. Add an `EDITORIAL NOTE` display row on the affected interlinear line.
3. Add a structured `editorial_notes` entry in the affected `source_lines` record.
4. In the public reader text, add a parenthetical correction only when the modern Zazaki display would otherwise point readers toward a likely wrong lexical form. Double-accent stress problems are normally note-only because the modern Zazaki spelling is usually unaffected.
5. Use a question mark in the reader parenthetical unless the correction has been externally confirmed.
