Lerch Glossary Candidate Review

Review helper for text forms that may need a new glossary entry, a merge into an existing entry, or an explicit ignore decision. Edits autosave in this browser.
Loaded
Candidate form is the normalized form seen in processed Lerch texts after broad matching against the current local glossary. It is not automatically a missing lemma.
Variants are the spellings/forms seen in the texts. Lemma hints are the current interlinear lemmas used to test whether this should merge with an existing glossary entry. Gloss hints are contextual glosses from the interlinears, not final dictionary definitions. Examples show where the form appears.
Use Add new glossary entry only when the form is not already covered. Use Merge with existing glossary entry when it belongs under a known headword such as a base word plus case/ezafe material. Use Do not add: inflected form when it is only a grammatical form that should stay out of the headword list.
