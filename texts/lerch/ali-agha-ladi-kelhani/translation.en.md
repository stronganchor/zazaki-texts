Ali Agha, Son of Kelhan

Ali Agha, son of Kelhan, was chief of the Karbegan district. His village was called Narbyes.

Ali Agha attacked four villages and killed thirty-four people. Nothing happened to him; no one's hand touched him.

Then Ali Agha packed up, left his house, and went to Syerature, where he stayed for thirty-six days. Thirty-four Karbegan villages and thirty-four Sivan villages gathered and held counsel.

The elders of Sivan, Qasim Agha of Karbegan, and Hasan Agha of Weshin came together and said, "Let us attack Ali Agha's house." Qasim Agha said, "We will trick Ali Agha. At night we will call out our army and gather it; at night we will go and kill Ali Agha and all four of his sons." Hasan Agha of Weshin said, "Good.

Let us go and kill Ali Agha." The Sivan elders said, "We will not take part." Qasim Agha said, "You are not needed." The Sivan elders withdrew and went home. But Qasim Agha and Hasan Agha set out to attack Ali Agha's house.

They took all four of his sons away and brought them to Mehmed Hendani's house. At night Qasim Agha sent word to the villages, saying, "Why are you waiting?

We are going to kill Ali Agha." The villagers gathered; at night they rose, and four hundred armed men came out and joined Qasim Agha. Qasim Agha mounted his horse, went at the head of the army, came to Xeyd, took another hundred men from Xeyd, and went with five hundred men to Ali Agha.

Qasim Agha went in to Ali Agha, sat down, and said, "Agha, I have come to ask you for weapons." Ali Agha said, "Agha, do not deceive me; I will not give you my weapons." Qasim Agha said, "Do not be afraid. I will not betray you." Ali Agha said, "You are a traitor.

I will not give you my weapons." Qasim Agha swore an oath. Ali Agha gathered his own weapons and the weapons of all four of his sons and gave them to Qasim Agha.

Qasim Agha took them. Ahmed, Ali Agha's son, said, "Uncle, do not take our weapons.

You are a traitor. Perhaps my father has no sense in his head; today is Ramadan.

But look, uncle: if you take our weapons and then go out as a traitor, I will kill you." Qasim Agha said to Ahmed, "Nephew, do not be afraid." Ahmed said, "Uncle, give me my dagger. I know you are a traitor; when you go out, you will kill us." The uncle did not give Ahmed his dagger.

The uncle went outside and said to his soldiers, "Do not delay. I have taken the weapons of Ali Agha and all four of his sons, and I have come out.

Do not delay. Break the door, go inside, and kill Ali Agha and all four of his sons.

There are also eighteen strangers with them; do not touch them." Qasim Agha's soldiers broke the door. Qasim Agha went ahead and called to Ahmed, "Nephew, I have come.

Wherever you go, I will cut off your head at once." Ahmed called back, "May God give me leave; I will kill you myself first." The uncle went to kill Ahmed. Ahmed saw an opening in the wall of the room, with a lattice in the wall.

He put his hand through the lattice, found a dagger behind it, drew it out, and struck the uncle straight in the right side of the chest. The uncle fell.

Ahmed pulled the dagger from the uncle and killed Eysan and Hasan Kalan. A sword struck Ahmed between the eyes, and his eyes filled with blood.

Ahmed became enraged and killed seven men with that dagger. Then Ahmed was killed.

All three of Ahmed's brothers and his father were killed. The eighteen strangers were also killed.

The armed men left, and everyone went home. The bodies of Ali Agha, all four of his sons, and the eighteen strangers remained together in the room.

In the morning Mela Resa called out to Mela Qasim of Desmund and said, "Bring the bodies of Ali Agha, his children, and the eighteen strangers here; bring them and carry them out." Mela Qasim set out from Desmun, Memed Agha set out from Xeyd, and Ramedan Agha set out from Merzyele. They went, took the bodies of Ali Agha, his sons, and the eighteen strangers, brought them away, carried them off, and buried them.
