Conversation with Hassan

How are you? I am well.

What is the name of your tribe? The name of our tribe is Sivan.

How many villages does your tribe have? Our tribe has thirty-four villages.

What are the names of your tribe's villages? Fatrakom, Hopsor, Tenik, Rezuan, Zimag, Horsig, Hemek, Bilike, Melekan, Mark, Aldun, Gewel, Kasan, Hoena, Sama, Emera, Shekera, Heylan, Mala-Ibrahiman, Avdelan, Mistan, Sayere, Abasa, Wisheyn, Haspeg, Seratyori, Akeragi, Letan, Gahar, Gowman, Kavare, Talek.

Who is the elder of the village of Aldun? Ali Beg is the elder of the village of Aldun.

Who is the elder of the village of Gewel? Mistefa Ali is the elder.

What is the name of your village? Kasan is my village.

How many houses are there in the village of Kasan? There are sixty houses in Kasan.

Do you have a garden? I have a garden.

What kind of trees does your garden have? It has trees: apple trees, apricot trees, peach trees, pomegranate trees and grapevines.

How many rooms are there in one house? Some people's houses have four or five rooms; some people have one house and no room.

Do you go to the summer pasture? We go to the summer pasture.

When do you go to the summer pasture? In the month of Temmuz we go to the summer pasture.

Do you live in black tents at the summer pasture? We live in black tents; there are houses, and we live in houses.

When do you come back down? We come back at the beginning of autumn.

Have you seen many feuds? I saw many feuds. By my father, I saw the feud between Nerib and Hyeni; I went to the feud between Nerib and Hyeni, and to the feud between Ahmed Beg and Sivan;

I was there. I saw the feud between Sivan and Avdula Beg; I was there. I saw the feud between Karbegan and Sivan; I was there, and the feud between Weshin and Sivan, I saw; I was there. Those were tribal feuds. I do not fear feuds.

Can you not tell the feud between Sivan and Avdula Beg? I am fasting; I cannot tell it; my heart wants a pipe.
